# You are the most fragmented database on earth

Count your accounts. Email, search, the phone and every app on it. Banks,
brokers, crypto exchanges, the mortgage, the insurance policies. Health
providers, the gym, the wearable, the pharmacy. Amazon, the grocery app,
the airline, the hotel chain. WhatsApp, Instagram, the gaming platforms.
The school portal, the car's telematics, the postal service, the utility
company. Every LLM provider you've ever typed a thought into.

Each one holds a shard of you. Each shard is complete enough to monetize
and partial enough to be useless for understanding your life. Your bank
knows what you spend but not why. Your health provider has your labs but
not your sleep, your food, or the stress that shows up in both. Amazon
knows what you bought; only you know which purchase was a mistake worth
remembering. ChatGPT knows what you asked in 2024; it doesn't know what
you eventually decided, because you decided it somewhere else.

**Every company on earth has a complete profile of their sliver of you.
Nobody — including you — has the whole person.**

## The joins are where your life actually is

A single shard answers a vendor's question: *will this user churn, click,
renew?* The joins answer *your* questions:

- Subscriptions you pay for **×** what you actually use → the audit nobody
  sends you.
- Everything with a renewal date **×** a calendar → the radar that catches
  the gym contract before it auto-renews and the passport before the trip.
- Order history **×** warranty PDFs **×** the receipt in your inbox → the
  dispute letter that writes itself.
- Labs **×** workouts **×** what you told your AI about how you slept →
  the health timeline no single provider can assemble.
- What you were told **×** what you decided **×** why → institutional
  memory of your own life.

No vendor can build these joins, because no vendor has the shards — and no
vendor *should*, because of what the joined thing is.

## Why the answer cannot be a cloud service

The assembled whole — money + health + location + conversations + trades +
family — is the most sensitive dataset that could exist about you. Handing
it to one company isn't a product; it's a hostage situation with a
subscription fee. Every previous attempt to "unify your life" quietly
became an advertising signal or an acquisition target, because aggregated
selves are exactly what the surveillance economy pays for.

This is the argument self-hosting was waiting for. Not ideology, not
nerd preference: **the whole-you dataset is only safe on hardware you
control, readable by models you point at it, in formats you can walk away
with.** The more complete the picture, the less anyone else can be trusted
to hold it — including us. That's why SovereignAI has no cloud, no
telemetry, no credential custody, and an [audited sovereignty ledger](SOVEREIGNTY.md)
instead of a trust-us page.

## What reassembly looks like

The mechanics already exist in this product, proven on the first shard —
your AI chat history:

1. **Export is a legal right.** GDPR and CCPA made nearly every service on
   your list hand over your data on request. The exports are ugly, slow,
   and all different — which is a parsing problem, and parsing problems
   are what the import architecture here was built for: bespoke parsers
   where formats are verifiable, a documented generic format for
   everything else, provenance on every record, idempotent re-runs.
2. **Distillation turns archives into understanding.** Imported data is
   inert until a model — yours, running where you decide — sweeps it for
   what matters durably, writing memory that names its source and its
   author.
3. **Provenance keeps it trustworthy.** A reassembled self is only useful
   if every claim in it can answer "says who?" Every memory here carries
   its origin, source, and authoring model; every export carries checksums.
4. **The exit is load-bearing.** The reassembled you rides in a documented,
   verifiable, optionally encrypted format — and distills into a portfolio
   you can paste into any AI you'll ever use. Reassembly without exit
   would just be one more shard-holder.

Today this works for AI chat history (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and a
generic format). The same rails extend shard by shard — the inbox first,
because receipts, statements, bookings, and renewals make email the
backdoor to half your life. That is the roadmap: not more chat, more of
*you*, reassembled where only you can read it.
